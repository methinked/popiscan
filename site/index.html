Why Browser-First POPIA Compliance?

PopiScan (popiscan.co.za) is built on a 100% zero-knowledge client architecture:

  • Zero Server Liability: Traditional face recognition requires uploading sensitive biometric images of children and staff to a cloud server, creating massive POPIA Section 19 security risk and data breach liability.
  • 100% Client-Side Processing: All facial feature detection, matching against your local roster, and privacy redaction happen strictly within your browser sandbox via WebAssembly and HTML5 Canvas.
  • Open Source & Auditable: The entire codebase is open source under GPLv3 and available for independent security audit on GitHub.
  • POPIA Section 35 Strict Adherence: Special personal information (biometric data of minors) is never transmitted across the network, stored in a remote database, or processed by third-party APIs.
  • Immediate Anonymization: Flagged non-consenting individuals can be blurred, masked, or pixelated in one click before event photos are published or shared.
  • Operational Assistance Only: Provided strictly "as-is" without warranty. See our Liability & Legal Disclaimer for full statutory terms.
