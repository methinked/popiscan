3. Inherent Algorithmic Limits & Probabilistic Computer Vision

You acknowledge and understand that client-side facial detection, feature tracking, and image analysis algorithms operate on statistical heuristics and mathematical approximations. They are fundamentally probabilistic and cannot achieve absolute certainty:

  • False Negatives (Undetected Faces): The scanner may fail to detect faces that are turned away from the camera, partially occluded, heavily shadowed, poorly illuminated, low resolution, motion blurred, wearing sunglasses, masks, religious headwear, or located in crowded background groups.
  • False Positives (Incorrect Flags): The scanner may identify inanimate objects, textures, foliage, or clothing patterns as human faces.
  • Matching Uncertainties: Local roster matching relies on localized feature comparison. Differences in age, hair style, angle, facial expression, and image quality can result in inaccurate matches or false non-matches.

Mandatory Human Review: Because automated algorithms cannot guarantee exhaustive detection, you must always perform rigorous human visual review before publishing, printing, distributing, or publicly uploading any event photos.
